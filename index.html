Privacy Policy for Alfonso's Flash Card Adventure

Effective date: September 10, 2026

Alfonso's Flash Card Adventure is developed by alfonsohluceroapps.

Information Collection

Alfonso's Flash Card Adventure does not require users to create an account and does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information.

Local Data

Flash cards, decks, study progress, and application settings are stored locally on the user's device and are used only to provide the app's functionality.

Internet and External Servers

The app is designed to operate offline and does not require a remote server or cloud account for its core features.

Third-Party Services

The current version of the app does not use advertising networks, analytics services, or third-party tracking technologies.

Data Deletion

Users can remove data created in the app using available app features. Uninstalling the application normally removes locally stored application data, subject to Android device backup and restore settings.

Children's Privacy

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users.

Changes to This Policy

This privacy policy may be updated if the application's features or data practices change. The current version will be made available on this webpage.
